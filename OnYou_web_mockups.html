OnYou web mockups

Every MVP screen twice: in a 1040-pixel desktop browser and on a 390-pixel phone. It is one responsive web app, so each phone screen is the same page with its layout adapted below 600 pixels wide.

Copy and behavior follow PRD v2.8: a session belongs to the account, every open OnYou tab shows it, and it ends early only on Give up, logout, or 30 seconds with no tab connected.

One apple today. One more 25-minute session fills Mochi up.

Accounts

Sign up, log in, and recover a forgotten password. On desktop Mochi greets people on the left and the form sits on the right; on phones Mochi sits above the form.

Dashboard and pet

Mochi beside today’s apples and total coins on desktop, stacked on phones. Apples reset at midnight; coins never do. While a session runs, every screen shows a bar that leads back to it.

Focus session

Start, count down, finish, or give up. The session belongs to the account, so every open OnYou tab shows the same countdown and any of them can finish it or give up. Results show coins, feeding minutes, and apples.

History and settings

History lists every session, newest first, 20 at a time: a table on desktop and cards on phones. Settings holds the pet’s name and logout.

Responsive web mockup for the OnYou MVP. On phones, dialogs become bottom sheets, buttons span the screen, the duration preview card is hidden, and inputs use 16-pixel text so the browser does not zoom. Colors and type are placeholders for the Tailwind theme: pine for actions, apple red for food, plum for giving up and errors. Pet art is original and drawn in code.
